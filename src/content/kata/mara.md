## What it is

Mara is Shizenryu's own kata — a structured solo routine created by Ian to give disciplined, Japanese-style solo training without the need to learn a long list of kata that often repeat the same movements. It focuses on the essential tools and body movements required for the partner work of Kumite. It is essentially a solo boxing drill that develops:

- Breath timing
- Balance
- Weight shifting
- Rotational movement
- Posture and alignment
- Flow of defence and counter strike
- Efficiency of movement

It began as a task: one solo karate routine to shape up a club strong in sensitivity but light on structure, drilled every Sunday until it earned its place in the syllabus. It is the first kata a Shizenryu student learns and the last one they stop refining.

## How it grows with you

Mara is learned in numbered stages that track the belts: **Mara 1–4** at 9th Kyu, **1–6** at 8th, **1–8** at 7th, **1–10** at 6th, **1–12** at 5th, and **complete** from 4th Kyu. From there the kata turns inward: 2nd Kyu asks for *mastery over subtle body torsion*; Shodan asks for *improved movement and transitions*. A full run-through takes about one minute.

## Study notes

From the earliest stages, practise **pulling back** and using **mae-geri to change sides**. Mara is not only solo work — it feeds directly into partner practice as the **Mara reflex flow drill** (Hansha Kumite), which must be shown complete from 2nd Kyu. If your Mara is hollow, your contact reflexes will be too.
